About this model

The LT-SS15300TPH extends the LEADTO bathroom accessory program with a bathroom hardware set designed for OEM and ODM orders alike. The item is cast with reinforced mounting points to survive commercial-grade installation and repeated maintenance cycles. Its water-saving geometry reduces per-use consumption without sacrificing flush performance, an easy point to make in green-building tenders. It is a frequent pick for residential developments, student housing and renovation programs. LEADTO supports OEM/ODM cooperation, from existing-mold customization to new-mold development.
