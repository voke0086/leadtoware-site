About this model

The LT-12067-BRA-PQVA is the kind of bidet sprayer that disappears into a project plan: standard dimensions, no surprises, ready to spec. Distributors typically stock it for mid-range residential and light-commercial demand. For quotations, container mix planning and after-sales parts, reach the LEADTO export team directly. Its ceramic body is glazed to a low-porosity finish, cutting water absorption and keeping the surface easier to maintain over years of service. OEM branding is available: logo casting, custom carton printing and packaging design can be arranged against volume commitments.
