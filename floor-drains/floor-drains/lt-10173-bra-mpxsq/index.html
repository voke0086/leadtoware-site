About this model

Suitable for both new-build installations and retrofit programs thanks to its standard interface dimensions. Manufactured in the Chaozhou sanitary-ware cluster, it benefits from mature kiln control and consistent glaze batches across large orders. The LT-10173-BRA-MPXSQ brings a floor drain to the LEADTO range, built around a durable ceramic body and an easy-to-clean glaze.
