About this model

LEADTO's LT-A71-100 answers the demand for space-saving washbasin storage: a 1000mm wall-hung vanity in moisture-resistant board, complete with mirror cabinet. Internal shelving takes care of towels and toiletries, and the basin deck is shaped to limit water splashing at the front edge. The factory finishes this unit in a white tone that coordinates with ceramic basins and brassware across the LEADTO range.
