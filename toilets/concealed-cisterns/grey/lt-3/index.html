About this model

Quality control covers water testing and glaze inspection before packing, with export-grade cartons and foam protection as standard. Its water-saving geometry reduces per-use consumption without sacrificing flush performance, an easy point to make in green-building tenders. As part of the LEADTO toilet series, the LT-dual-round-button-gun-grey targets importers who need consistent quality across container-size orders. LEADTO supports OEM/ODM cooperation, from existing-mold customization to new-mold development. Suitable for both new-build installations and retrofit programs thanks to its standard interface dimensions. A soft grey tone gives the piece a contemporary feel while staying neutral enough for mixed-tile schemes.
