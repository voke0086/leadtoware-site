About this model

Drainage is via an S-trap for floor connection, the default in most renovation and mid-market projects. The glaze is applied evenly across the trapway and interior surfaces, reducing limescale build-up in hard-water installations. Sample lead time is short; bulk orders are scheduled against the production calendar with mixed-container support. Suitable for both new-build installations and retrofit programs thanks to its standard interface dimensions. Its water-saving geometry reduces per-use consumption without sacrificing flush performance, an easy point to make in green-building tenders. The colored glaze is part of the firing process, delivering a saturated, durable finish for feature washrooms. Dimensions: 550x380x580 mm — confirm against your fit-out drawings before ordering samples. The LT-1685-CG-BU is a color-glazed children toilet that pairs a clean silhouette with practical detailing for everyday commercial use.
