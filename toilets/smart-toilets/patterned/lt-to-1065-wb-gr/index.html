About this model

First impressions in a washroom come from silhouette and glaze; the LT-TO-1065-WB-GR delivers both in a smart toilet format. The design keeps exposed surfaces smooth and ledge-free, leaving fewer places for dirt to settle. The listed footprint of 680x390x475 mm fits typical commercial washroom modules. Quality control covers water testing and glaze inspection before packing, with export-grade cartons and foam protection as standard. Distributors typically stock it for mid-range residential and light-commercial demand. Trade buyers can request the full specification sheet, packing details and test reports on request. The surface carries a kiln-fired decorative pattern, giving each unit slight variations that read as crafted rather than printed. It is an S-trap (floor outlet) model, suited to floor-mounted drainage at 300 mm or 400 mm set-out.

Quotation on request. Send us this model number together with your target quantity and destination port — we reply with unit price, carton dimensions, gross weight and lead time. Mixed-SKU container loading and private-label packing are available.
