About this model

The body is fired vitreous china with a fully glazed surface that resists staining and holds its finish under daily cleaning chemicals. Suitable for both new-build installations and retrofit programs thanks to its standard interface dimensions. LEADTO ships ex-factory Chaozhou with mixed-SKU container loading and full export documentation. Compact where it matters, the LT-TO-1135-WA-WG smart toilet works in tight washrooms as well as full-fit commercial projects. The design keeps exposed surfaces smooth and ledge-free, leaving fewer places for dirt to settle. Dimensions: 720x420x470 mm — confirm against your fit-out drawings before ordering samples. A soft grey tone gives the piece a contemporary feel while staying neutral enough for mixed-tile schemes.
