About this model

It comes with a P-trap for wall-hung drainage runs, common in modern European-style fit-outs. A soft grey tone gives the piece a contemporary feel while staying neutral enough for mixed-tile schemes. For buyers sourcing toilet in volume, the LT-TO-1020-WB-MDG keeps the specification simple: grey finish, smart toilet layout, factory-direct lead times. Manufactured in the Chaozhou sanitary-ware cluster, it benefits from mature kiln control and consistent glaze batches across large orders.
