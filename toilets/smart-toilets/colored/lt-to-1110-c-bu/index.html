About this model

Contact the LEADTO sales desk for FOB pricing, mold customization and container loading plans. The LT-TO-1110-C-BU is a color-glazed smart toilet that pairs a clean silhouette with practical detailing for everyday commercial use. Typical applications include hotels, serviced apartments, office washrooms and public facilities. It comes with a P-trap for wall-hung drainage runs, common in modern European-style fit-outs. The colored glaze is part of the firing process, delivering a saturated, durable finish for feature washrooms. The design keeps exposed surfaces smooth and ledge-free, leaving fewer places for dirt to settle. Its ceramic body is glazed to a low-porosity finish, cutting water absorption and keeping the surface easier to maintain over years of service. The listed footprint of 680x390x440 mm fits typical commercial washroom modules.
