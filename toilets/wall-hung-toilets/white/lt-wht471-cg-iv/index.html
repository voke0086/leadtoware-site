About this model

Finished in classic white, it integrates with any tile palette and remains the safest specification for volume projects. The LT-WHT471-CG-IV is a white wall-hung toilet that pairs a clean silhouette with practical detailing for everyday commercial use. The listed footprint of 510x348x360 mm fits typical commercial washroom modules. It comes with a P-trap for wall-hung drainage runs, common in modern European-style fit-outs. The item is built with reinforced mounting points to survive commercial-grade installation and repeated maintenance cycles. Specifiers use it across hospitality, healthcare and education projects where maintenance cost matters. LEADTO ships from our factory Chaozhou with mixed-SKU container loading and full export documentation. Its ceramic body is glazed to a low-porosity finish, cutting water absorption and keeping the surface easier to maintain over years of service.
