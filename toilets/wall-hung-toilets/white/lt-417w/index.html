About this model

Its ceramic body is glazed to a low-porosity finish, cutting water absorption and keeping the surface easier to maintain over years of service. Trade buyers can request the full specification sheet, packing details and test reports on request. At 500x365x380 mm, it drops into standard installation frames without on-site modification. It is a frequent pick for residential developments, student housing and renovation programs.
