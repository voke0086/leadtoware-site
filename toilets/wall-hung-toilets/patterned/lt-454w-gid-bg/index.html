About this model

The surface carries a kiln-fired decorative pattern, giving each unit slight variations that read as crafted rather than printed. The LT-454W-GID-BG is a decor-patterned wall-hung toilet that pairs a clean silhouette with practical detailing for everyday commercial use. The design keeps exposed surfaces smooth and ledge-free, leaving fewer places for dirt to settle. It comes with a P-trap for wall-hung drainage runs, common in modern European-style fit-outs. Suitable for both new-build installations and retrofit programs thanks to its standard interface dimensions. Dimensions: 510x345x340 mm — confirm against your fit-out drawings before ordering samples. Manufactured in the Chaozhou sanitary-ware cluster, it benefits from mature kiln control and consistent glaze batches across large orders.
