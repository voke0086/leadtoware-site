About this model

It comes with a P-trap for wall-hung drainage runs, common in modern European-style fit-outs. Contact the LEADTO sales desk for FOB pricing, mold customization and container loading plans. A practical wall-hung toilet option from our toilet line, the LT-450W-M-AG is aimed at hotels, apartments and public facilities. Specifiers use it across hospitality, healthcare and education projects where maintenance cost matters. The glaze is applied evenly across the trapway and interior surfaces, reducing limescale build-up in hard-water installations. At 480x370x370 mm, it drops into standard installation frames without on-site modification. The surface carries a kiln-fired decorative pattern, giving each unit slight variations that read as crafted rather than printed. OEM branding is available: logo casting, custom carton printing and packaging design can be arranged against volume commitments.

Quotation on request. Send us this model number together with your target quantity and destination port — we reply with unit price, carton dimensions, gross weight and lead time. Mixed-SKU container loading and private-label packing are available.
