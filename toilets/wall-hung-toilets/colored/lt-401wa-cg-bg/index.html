About this model

LEADTO ships ex-factory Chaozhou with mixed-SKU container loading and full export documentation. Suitable for both new-build installations and retrofit programs thanks to its standard interface dimensions. Overall dimensions are 540x360x365 mm, matching the common roughing-in used across Asia, Africa and Latin America projects. A P-trap (wall outlet) version handles wall-spigot drainage at a 180 mm set-out. The colored glaze is part of the firing process, delivering a saturated, durable finish for feature washrooms. Its ceramic body is glazed to a low-porosity finish, cutting water absorption and keeping the surface easier to maintain over years of service. Compact where it matters, the LT-401WA-CG-BG wall-hung toilet works in tight washrooms as well as full-fit commercial projects. The body is fired vitreous china with a fully glazed surface that resists staining and holds its finish under daily cleaning chemicals.
