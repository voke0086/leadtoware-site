About this model

Typical applications include hotels, serviced apartments, office washrooms and public facilities. The colored glaze is part of the firing process, delivering a saturated, durable finish for feature washrooms. Fittings are standard-sized, so replacement parts are easy to source locally in any market. At 25x265x20 mm, it drops into standard installation frames without on-site modification. Its water-saving geometry reduces per-use consumption without sacrificing flush performance, an easy point to make in green-building tenders. The LT-WHT4029-GID-M-BLRG3 is a color-glazed wall-hung toilet that pairs a clean silhouette with practical detailing for everyday commercial use.
