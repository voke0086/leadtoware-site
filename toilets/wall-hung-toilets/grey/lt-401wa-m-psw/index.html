About this model

The body is fired vitreous china with a fully glazed surface that resists staining and holds its finish under daily cleaning chemicals. For quotations, container mix planning and after-sales parts, reach the LEADTO export team directly. A P-trap (wall outlet) version handles wall-spigot drainage at a 180 mm set-out. Specifiers use it across hospitality, healthcare and education projects where maintenance cost matters. Overall dimensions are 540x360x365 mm, matching the common sizes used across Asia, Africa and Latin America projects. The LT-401WA-M-PSW extends the LEADTO toilet program with a grey wall-hung toilet designed for OEM and ODM orders alike. Fittings are standard-sized, so replacement parts are easy to source locally in any market. A soft grey tone gives the piece a contemporary feel while staying neutral enough for mixed-tile schemes.
