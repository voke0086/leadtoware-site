About this model

For buyers sourcing toilet in volume, the LT-WHT4081-CG-LG keeps the specification simple: grey finish, wall-hung toilet layout, factory-direct lead times. The listed footprint of 520x370x350 mm fits typical commercial washroom modules. The glaze is applied evenly across the trapway and interior surfaces, reducing limescale build-up in hard-water installations. A soft grey tone gives the piece a contemporary feel while staying neutral enough for mixed-tile schemes. Sample lead time is short; bulk orders are scheduled against the production calendar with mixed-container support. A P-trap (wall outlet) version handles wall-spigot drainage at a 180 mm set-out. The item is cast with reinforced mounting points to survive commercial-grade installation and repeated maintenance cycles. Specifiers use it across hospitality, healthcare and education projects where maintenance cost matters.
