About this model

The design keeps exposed surfaces smooth and ledge-free, leaving fewer places for dirt to settle. The LT-401WA-DP-CT4 extends the LEADTO toilet program with a grey wall-hung toilet designed for OEM and ODM orders alike. A P-trap (wall outlet) version handles wall-spigot drainage at a 180 mm set-out. For quotations, container mix planning and after-sales parts, reach the LEADTO export team directly. The item is built with reinforced mounting points to survive commercial-grade installation and repeated maintenance cycles. It is a frequent pick for residential developments, student housing and renovation programs. Overall dimensions are 540x360x365 mm, matching the common sizes used across Asia, Africa and Latin America projects.
