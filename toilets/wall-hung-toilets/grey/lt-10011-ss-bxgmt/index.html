About this model

Typical applications include hotels, serviced apartments, office washrooms and public facilities. A soft grey tone gives the piece a contemporary feel while staying neutral enough for mixed-tile schemes. Defined by straight lines and a reassuring footprint, the LT-10011-SS-BXGMT wall-hung toilet suits projects that need volume without visual noise. For quotations, container mix planning and after-sales parts, reach the LEADTO export team directly. Manufactured in the Chaozhou sanitary-ware cluster, it benefits from mature kiln control and consistent glaze batches across large orders. The glaze is applied evenly across the trapway and interior surfaces, reducing limescale build-up in hard-water installations.
