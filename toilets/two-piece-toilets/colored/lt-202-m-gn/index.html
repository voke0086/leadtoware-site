About this model

Trade buyers can request the full specification sheet, packing details and test reports on request. A P-trap (wall outlet) version handles wall-spigot drainage at a 180 mm set-out. The LT-202-M-GN extends the LEADTO toilet program with a color-glazed two-piece toilet designed for OEM and ODM orders alike. The colored glaze is part of the firing process, delivering a saturated, durable finish for feature washrooms. Its water-saving geometry reduces per-use consumption without sacrificing flush performance, an easy point to make in green-building tenders. Manufactured in the Chaozhou sanitary-ware cluster, it benefits from mature kiln control and consistent glaze batches across large orders. Distributors typically stock it for mid-range residential and light-commercial demand.
