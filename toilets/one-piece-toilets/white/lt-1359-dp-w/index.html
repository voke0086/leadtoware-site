About this model

Suitable for both new-build installations and retrofit programs thanks to its standard interface dimensions. The LT-1359-DP-W is a white one-piece toilet that pairs a clean silhouette with practical detailing for everyday commercial use. Fittings are standard-sized, so replacement parts and seat accessories are easy to source locally in any market. OEM branding is available: logo casting, custom carton printing and packaging design can be arranged against volume commitments. Finished in classic white, it integrates with any tile palette and remains the safest specification for volume projects. Overall dimensions are 700x400x580 mm, matching the common roughing-in used across Asia, Africa and Latin America projects. LEADTO ships ex-factory Chaozhou with mixed-SKU container loading and full export documentation.
