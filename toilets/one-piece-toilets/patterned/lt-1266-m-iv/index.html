About this model

Drainage is via an S-trap for floor connection, the default in most renovation and mid-market projects. A practical one-piece toilet option from our toilet line, the LT-1266-M-IV is aimed at hotels, apartments and public facilities. The design keeps exposed surfaces smooth and ledge-free, leaving fewer places for dirt to settle. The surface carries a kiln-fired decorative pattern, giving each unit slight variations that read as crafted rather than printed. Dimensions: 700x470x635 mm — confirm against your fit-out drawings before ordering samples. Distributors typically stock it for mid-range residential and light-commercial demand. Fittings are standard-sized, so replacement parts are easy to source locally in any market. LEADTO ships from our factory Chaozhou with mixed-SKU container loading and full export documentation.
