About this model

Its ceramic body is glazed to a low-porosity finish, cutting water absorption and keeping the surface easier to maintain over years of service. First impressions in a washroom come from silhouette and glaze; the LT-1359-CG-IVGLW delivers both in a one-piece toilet format. Dimensions: 700x400x580 mm — confirm against your fit-out drawings before ordering samples. Sample lead time is short; bulk orders are scheduled against the production calendar with mixed-container support. Distributors typically stock it for mid-range residential and light-commercial demand. The glaze is applied evenly across the trapway and interior surfaces, reducing limescale build-up in hard-water installations.
