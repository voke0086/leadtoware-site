About this model

Its water-saving geometry reduces per-use consumption without sacrificing flush performance, an easy point to make in green-building tenders. Typical applications include hotels, serviced apartments, office washrooms and public facilities. The body is fired vitreous china with a fully glazed surface that resists staining and holds its finish under daily cleaning chemicals. A practical overhead shower option from our shower line, the LT-10044-PLA-DP is aimed at hotels, apartments and public facilities.
