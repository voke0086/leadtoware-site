About this model

For quotations, container mix planning and after-sales parts, reach the LEADTO export team directly. Finished in classic white, it integrates with any tile palette and remains the safest specification for volume projects. Defined by straight lines and a reassuring footprint, the LT-450W-M-BL-LT-501-M-BL wall-hung toilet suite suits projects that need volume without visual noise.
