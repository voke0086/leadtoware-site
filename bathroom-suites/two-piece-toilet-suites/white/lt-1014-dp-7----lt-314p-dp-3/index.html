About this model

LEADTO supports OEM/ODM cooperation, from existing-mold customization to new-mold development. Finished in classic white, it integrates with any tile palette and remains the safest specification for volume projects. The LT-1014-DP-7-LT-314P-DP-3 extends the LEADTO bathroom suite program with a white two-piece toilet suite designed for OEM and ODM orders alike. OEM branding is available: logo casting, custom carton printing and packaging design can be arranged against volume commitments. The design keeps exposed surfaces smooth and ledge-free, leaving fewer places for dirt to settle. Typical applications include hotels, serviced apartments, office washrooms and public facilities.
