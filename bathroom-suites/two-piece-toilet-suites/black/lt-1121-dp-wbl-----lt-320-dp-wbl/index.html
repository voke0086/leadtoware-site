About this model

The LT-1121-DP-WBL-LT-320-DP-WBL answers a common project brief: a black two-piece toilet suite that installs fast, cleans faster, and ships straight from the Chaozhou production area. Its water-saving geometry reduces per-use consumption without sacrificing flush performance, an easy point to make in green-building tenders. The deep black glaze is fired into the body, not painted, so the color will not chip or fade with abrasive cleaning. Fittings are standard-sized, so replacement parts and seat accessories are easy to source locally in any market. Typical applications include hotels, serviced apartments, office washrooms and public facilities. Trade buyers can request the full specification sheet, packing details and test reports on request.
