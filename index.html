Why buyers work with us

23,954 models from one source

Toilets, basins, bathtubs, urinals, bathroom suites and fittings. Consolidate a full container from one supplier instead of several.

Mixed container loading

Combine models, shapes and finishes in one container. We plan the load and hand you the carton list before shipment.

OEM and ODM

Your brand on the product and on the carton. Custom shapes developed from your drawings.

Six finish families

White, black, grey, gold, coloured and patterned. Finish is checked visually before packing.

Certification for your market

Tell us the destination and we match the paperwork to it. Some models already hold the certificates your market asks for; where they do not, we arrange testing on the model you pick. Certificates are issued to a plant and to a specific model, so we state in writing which one covers the model in your quotation — never a blanket claim across the whole range.

Models that already hold the certificate

North America: cUPC / UPC and WaterSense. European Union: CE with EN 997. Saudi Arabia: SASO and SABER. Australia: WaterMark and WELS. Brazil: INMETRO. Russia and Eurasia: EAC. Korea: KC. Tell us the destination and we shortlist the models that already carry the mark.

Testing arranged on the model you choose

If you prefer a specific model, we submit it to the required laboratory and follow the process through to the certificate. Laboratory, scope and timing are confirmed for that model before anything starts.

Documents that travel with the goods

Certificate copies, test reports, packing list and certificate of origin are prepared with every shipment, so clearance and project handover do not stall on paperwork.

Stated model by model

We do not print a certification mark on a model that has not been tested. What applies to your order is written on the quotation, one model at a time.

Questions buyers ask

Are you a factory or a trading company?
We are a manufacturer. Production runs in Fengtang, Chao'an — the Chaozhou district behind the largest share of China's sanitary ware output. Inspection, documentation and shipping are handled by the same team, so you deal with the factory rather than through a trading layer.
Can I mix different models in one container?
Yes. Mixing models, shapes and finishes in one container is normal for us. Tell us your target mix and we return a loading plan with carton counts and CBM before you confirm.
What is your minimum order quantity?
One mixed container is the usual starting point. We do not set a per-model minimum inside it — you can take a few cartons of a slow-moving shape and more of the volume lines.
What is the lead time?
15 to 25 days from deposit, depending on firing cycles and order size. The exact date is confirmed in writing when the order is placed.
Which certifications do your products hold?
It depends on the model and the destination market. Tell us where the goods are going and we will state exactly which certificates the quoted model currently holds. We do not print "certified" on a model that has not been tested.
