About this model

Its water-saving geometry reduces per-use consumption without sacrificing flush performance, an easy point to make in green-building tenders. Distributors typically stock it for mid-range residential and light-commercial demand. Quality control covers water testing and glaze inspection before packing, with export-grade cartons and foam protection as standard. For quotations, container mix planning and after-sales parts, reach the LEADTO export team directly. Built for high-traffic washrooms, the LT-10015-CFE-PJ balances a surface with a form factor that fits standard roughing-in.
