About this model

Built for high-traffic washrooms, the LT-10055-MA-YG balances a color-glazed surface with a form factor that fits standard roughing-in. Overall dimensions are 1720x860x680 mm, matching the common roughing-in used across Asia, Africa and Latin America projects. Specifiers use it across hospitality, healthcare and education projects where maintenance cost matters. Fittings are standard-sized, so replacement parts and seat accessories are easy to source locally in any market. For quotations, container mix planning and after-sales parts, reach the LEADTO export team directly. The body is fired vitreous china with a fully glazed surface that resists staining and holds its finish under daily cleaning chemicals.
