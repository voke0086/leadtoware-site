About this model

The LT-10028-CFE-YCXZZ1 extends the LEADTO faucet program with a bathtub faucet designed for OEM and ODM orders alike. Its water-saving geometry reduces per-use consumption without sacrificing flush performance, an easy point to make in green-building tenders. Suitable for both new-build installations and retrofit programs thanks to its standard interface dimensions. Sample lead time is short; bulk orders are scheduled against the production calendar with mixed-container support. The body is fired vitreous china with a fully glazed surface that resists staining and holds its finish under daily cleaning chemicals.

Quotation on request. Send us this model number together with your target quantity and destination port — we reply with unit price, carton dimensions, gross weight and lead time. Mixed-SKU container loading and private-label packing are available.
