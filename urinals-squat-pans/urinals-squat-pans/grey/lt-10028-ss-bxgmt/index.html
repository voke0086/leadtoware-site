About this model

Specifiers use it across hospitality, healthcare and education projects where maintenance cost matters. Its ceramic body is glazed to a low-porosity finish, cutting water absorption and keeping the surface easier to maintain over years of service. For quotations, container mix planning and after-sales parts, reach the LEADTO export team directly. The surface carries a kiln-fired decorative pattern, giving each unit slight variations that read as crafted rather than printed. Manufactured in the Chaozhou sanitary-ware cluster, it benefits from mature kiln control and consistent glaze batches across large orders. Compact where it matters, the LT-10028-SS-BXGMT urinal and squat pan works in tight washrooms as well as full-fit commercial projects.
