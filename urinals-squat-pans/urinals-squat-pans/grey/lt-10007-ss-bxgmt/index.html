About this model

LEADTO supports OEM/ODM cooperation, from existing-mold customization to new-mold development. The surface carries a kiln-fired decorative pattern, giving each unit slight variations that read as crafted rather than printed. The LT-10007-SS-BXGMT is a decor-patterned urinal and squat pan that pairs a clean silhouette with practical detailing for everyday commercial use. It is a frequent pick for residential developments, student housing and renovation programs. The glaze is applied evenly across the trapway and interior surfaces, reducing limescale build-up in hard-water installations. Its ceramic body is glazed to a low-porosity finish, cutting water absorption and keeping the surface easier to maintain over years of service.
