About this model

Quality control covers water testing and glaze inspection before packing, with export-grade cartons and foam protection as standard. It is a frequent pick for residential developments, student housing and renovation programs. LEADTO supports OEM/ODM cooperation, from existing-mold customization to new-mold development. Its ceramic body is glazed to a low-porosity finish, cutting water absorption and keeping the surface easier to maintain over years of service. First impressions in a washroom come from silhouette and glaze; the LT-602-ABSwhite delivers both in a flush valve format.
