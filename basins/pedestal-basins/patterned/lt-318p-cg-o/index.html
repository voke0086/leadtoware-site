About this model

Typical applications include hotels, serviced apartments, office washrooms and public facilities. The LT-318P-CG-O is the kind of decor-patterned pedestal basin that disappears into a project plan: standard dimensions, no surprises, ready to spec. LEADTO ships from our factory Chaozhou with mixed-SKU container loading and full export documentation. The glaze is applied evenly across the trapway and interior surfaces, reducing limescale build-up in hard-water installations. The surface carries a kiln-fired decorative pattern, giving each unit slight variations that read as crafted rather than printed. The design keeps exposed surfaces smooth and ledge-free, leaving fewer places for dirt to settle. Overall dimensions are 25x820 mm, matching the common sizes used across Asia, Africa and Latin America projects.
