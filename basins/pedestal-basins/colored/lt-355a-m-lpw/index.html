About this model

Its water-saving geometry reduces per-use consumption without sacrificing flush performance, an easy point to make in green-building tenders. First impressions in a washroom come from silhouette and finish; the LT-355A-M-LPW delivers both in a pedestal basin format. The listed footprint of 625x420x845 mm fits typical commercial washroom modules. Typical applications include hotels, serviced apartments, office washrooms and public facilities. Sample lead time is short; bulk orders are scheduled against the production calendar with mixed-container support. Its ceramic body is glazed to a low-porosity finish, cutting water absorption and keeping the surface easier to maintain over years of service. The colored glaze is part of the firing process, delivering a saturated, durable finish for feature washrooms.
