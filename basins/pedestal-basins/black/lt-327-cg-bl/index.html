About this model

The deep black glaze is fired into the body, not painted, so the color will not chip or fade with abrasive cleaning. At 410x410x840 mm, it drops into standard installation frames without on-site modification. LEADTO supports OEM/ODM cooperation, from existing-mold customization to new-mold development. The design keeps exposed surfaces smooth and ledge-free, leaving fewer places for dirt to settle. First impressions in a washroom come from silhouette and finish; the LT-327-CG-BL delivers both in a pedestal basin format. Manufactured in the Chaozhou sanitary-ware cluster, it benefits from mature kiln control and consistent glaze batches across large orders. Typical applications include hotels, serviced apartments, office washrooms and public facilities.
