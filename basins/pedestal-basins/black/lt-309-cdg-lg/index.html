About this model

Its ceramic body is glazed to a low-porosity finish, cutting water absorption and keeping the surface easier to maintain over years of service. Specifiers use it across hospitality, healthcare and education projects where maintenance cost matters. The body is fired vitreous china with a fully glazed surface that resists staining and holds its finish under daily cleaning chemicals. Overall dimensions are 400x400x850 mm, matching the common sizes used across Asia, Africa and Latin America projects. The deep black glaze is fired into the body, not painted, so the color will not chip or fade with abrasive cleaning. The LT-309-CDG-LG answers a common project brief: a black pedestal basin that installs fast, cleans faster, and ships straight from the Chaozhou production area. Sample lead time is short; bulk orders are scheduled against the production calendar with mixed-container support.
