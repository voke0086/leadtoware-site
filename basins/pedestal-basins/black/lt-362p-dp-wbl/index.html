About this model

Its water-saving geometry reduces per-use consumption without sacrificing flush performance, an easy point to make in green-building tenders. Fittings are standard-sized, so replacement parts and seat accessories are easy to source locally in any market. The deep black glaze is fired into the body, not painted, so the color will not chip or fade with abrasive cleaning. Dimensions: 560x470x805 mm — confirm against your fit-out drawings before ordering samples. Built for high-traffic washrooms, the LT-362P-DP-WBL balances a black surface with a form factor that fits standard roughing-in. Trade buyers can request the full specification sheet, packing details and test reports on request. Specifiers use it across hospitality, healthcare and education projects where maintenance cost matters.
