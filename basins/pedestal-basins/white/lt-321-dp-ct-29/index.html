About this model

Finished in classic white, it integrates with any tile palette and remains the safest specification for volume projects. The design keeps exposed surfaces smooth and ledge-free, leaving fewer places for dirt to settle. The body is fired vitreous china with a fully glazed surface that resists staining and holds its finish under daily cleaning chemicals. Typical applications include hotels, serviced apartments, office washrooms and public facilities. Trade buyers can request the full specification sheet, packing details and test reports on request. Built for high-traffic washrooms, the LT-321-DP-CT-29 balances a white surface with a form factor that fits standard installation dimensions.
