About this model

The LT-501-HE-GBL1 answers a common project brief: a gold-tone wall-hung basin that installs fast, cleans faster, and ships straight from the Chaozhou production area. The glaze is applied evenly across the trapway and interior surfaces, reducing limescale build-up in hard-water installations. Sample lead time is short; bulk orders are scheduled against the production calendar with mixed-container support. Distributors typically stock it for mid-range residential and light-commercial demand. The design keeps exposed surfaces smooth and ledge-free, leaving fewer places for dirt to settle. Accent detailing in a warm gold finish positions this model for hotel and premium residential projects. Overall dimensions are 520x435x385 mm, matching the common roughing-in used across Asia, Africa and Latin America projects.
