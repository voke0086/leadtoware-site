About this model

The LT-501-M-LG is a grey wall-hung basin that pairs a clean silhouette with practical detailing for everyday commercial use. Trade buyers can request the full specification sheet, packing details and test reports on request. Typical applications include hotels, serviced apartments, office washrooms and public facilities. Its water-saving geometry reduces per-use consumption without sacrificing flush performance, an easy point to make in green-building tenders. The listed footprint of 520x435x385 mm fits typical commercial washroom modules. The body is fired vitreous china with a fully glazed surface that resists staining and holds its finish under daily cleaning chemicals.
