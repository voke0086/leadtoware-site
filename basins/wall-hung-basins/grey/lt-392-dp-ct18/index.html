About this model

LEADTO supports OEM/ODM cooperation, from existing-mold customization to new-mold development. The glaze is applied evenly across the trapway and interior surfaces, reducing limescale build-up in hard-water installations. Built for high-traffic washrooms, the LT-392-DP-CT18 balances a grey surface with a form factor that fits standard roughing-in. Suitable for both new-build installations and retrofit programs thanks to its standard interface dimensions. Overall dimensions are 420x265x840 mm, matching the common roughing-in used across Asia, Africa and Latin America projects. A soft grey tone gives the piece a contemporary feel while staying neutral enough for mixed-tile schemes. Its ceramic body is glazed to a low-porosity finish, cutting water absorption and keeping the surface easier to maintain over years of service.
