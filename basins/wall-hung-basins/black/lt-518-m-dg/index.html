About this model

The listed footprint of 565x490x380 mm fits typical commercial washroom modules. The item is cast with reinforced mounting points to survive commercial-grade installation and repeated maintenance cycles. Distributors typically stock it for mid-range residential and light-commercial demand. The LT-518-M-DG is a black wall-hung basin that pairs a clean silhouette with practical detailing for everyday commercial use. The deep black glaze is fired into the body, not painted, so the color will not chip or fade with abrasive cleaning.
