About this model

The LT-6517-DP-B brings a black art basin to the LEADTO range, built around durable construction and an easy-to-clean surface. Fittings are standard-sized, so replacement parts are easy to source locally in any market. LEADTO ships from our factory Chaozhou with mixed-SKU container loading and full export documentation. The deep black glaze is fired into the body, not painted, so the color will not chip or fade with abrasive cleaning. Dimensions: 400x400x145 mm — confirm against your fit-out drawings before ordering samples. It is a frequent pick for residential developments, student housing and renovation programs. Its ceramic body is glazed to a low-porosity finish, cutting water absorption and keeping the surface easier to maintain over years of service.
