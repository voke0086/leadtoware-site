About this model

For buyers sourcing basin in volume, the LT-6120-M-BL keeps the specification simple: black finish, art basin layout, factory-direct lead times. Trade buyers can request the full specification sheet, packing details and test reports on request. The deep black glaze is fired into the body, not painted, so the color will not chip or fade with abrasive cleaning. The design keeps exposed surfaces smooth and ledge-free, leaving fewer places for dirt to settle. The listed footprint of 420x420x130 mm fits typical commercial washroom modules. Distributors typically stock it for mid-range residential and light-commercial demand. Fittings are standard-sized, so replacement parts and seat accessories are easy to source locally in any market.
