About this model

Trade buyers can request the full specification sheet, packing details and test reports on request. Specifiers use it across hospitality, healthcare and education projects where maintenance cost matters. Its water-saving geometry reduces per-use consumption without sacrificing flush performance, an easy point to make in green-building tenders. Accent detailing in a warm gold finish positions this model for hotel and premium residential projects. Overall dimensions are 500x380x140 mm, matching the common roughing-in used across Asia, Africa and Latin America projects. First impressions in a washroom come from silhouette and glaze; the LT-6074-M-GR delivers both in a art basin format. The body is fired vitreous china with a fully glazed surface that resists staining and holds its finish under daily cleaning chemicals.
